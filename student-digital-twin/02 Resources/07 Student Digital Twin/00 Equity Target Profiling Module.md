An **Equity Target Profiling Module** is an institutional data management and decision-support tool designed for Higher Education Institutions (HEIs) to identify, categorize, monitor, and support marginalized and underrepresented student populations.

In the context of the **Commission on Higher Education (CHED)** and related national mandates—such as Republic Act No. 10931 (Universal Access to Quality Tertiary Education Act) and Republic Act No. 10687 (UniFAST Act)—this module aligns institutional admissions, retention, and student assistance with national equity targets.
