Automatically formats and extracts data required for annual CHED institutional data collection:

- **Form E-1 / E-2:** Institutional and program profile.
    
- **Form E-3:** Enrollment statistics categorized by program, major, year level, and sex.
    
- **Form E-4:** Graduate statistics by program and academic year.
    
- **Form E-5:** Faculty profile, employment status, teaching loads, and educational qualifications.
